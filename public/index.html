ABOUT

SurgiBoard Global

A global, evidence-mapped General Surgery learning platform for board candidates and postgraduate trainees.

Clinical content governance

Draft → evidence checked → item reviewed → specialist reviewed → published → retired. Each item carries provenance, version, references, reviewers, and review dates.

Copyright policy

Commercial MCQ books guide coverage and style. Protected items are not copied or closely paraphrased without written permission.

Privacy and security

Student records are private by default. Authorization is server-enforced, access is least-privilege, and administrator actions are auditable.

Medical disclaimer

For professional education and examination preparation only; not clinical decision support or patient-specific advice.
